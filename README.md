# 📊 Olist E-Commerce Analytics | End-to-End Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset** to identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, understand customer behaviour, and generate actionable business insights.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics approach using **MySQL, Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and Tableau** to transform raw data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, SQL analysis, KPI development, and interactive dashboards.

The primary objective is to help businesses understand their sales performance, product demand, customer satisfaction, payment preferences, and delivery operations to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing product categories and geographical regions.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns and distribution.
- Evaluate payment methods and transaction performance.
- Analyze customer satisfaction through review ratings.
- Monitor order status and delivery performance.
- Develop interactive dashboards to communicate business insights.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset**, containing approximately 100,000 orders from 2016 to 2018.

The dataset consists of 8 relational tables:

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| Customers | Customer IDs, cities, states, and geographical details |
| Orders | Order status and purchase/delivery timestamps |
| Order Items | Product details, seller information, price, and freight |
| Payments | Payment types, installments, and payment values |
| Reviews | Customer ratings and feedback |
| Products | Product categories and product attributes |
| Sellers | Seller IDs and geographical information |
| Geolocation | ZIP codes, latitude, longitude, cities, and states |

**Dataset Source:** [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Collected and organized the Olist e-commerce datasets.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Identified missing values, duplicate records, and data inconsistencies.
3. **Data Preparation:** Prepared datasets and handled data types and date columns.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Performed relational joins, aggregations, and business queries using MySQL.
5. **Excel Analysis:** Created Pivot Tables, calculated KPIs, and performed supporting analysis.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Built interactive dashboards using Power BI and Tableau.
7. **Business Insights:** Identified key trends and developed recommendations based on the analysis.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Sales Performance:** The project recorded approximately R$13.59 million in total sales, providing an overview of marketplace performance.

2. **Geographical Analysis:** São Paulo emerged as the leading state in the displayed sales analysis, highlighting its importance to overall business revenue.

3. **Product Performance:** Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, and Bed/Bath/Table were among the leading product categories by sales.

4. **Payment Preferences:** Credit cards represented the largest share of payment value, followed by other payment methods such as boleto.

5. **Customer Satisfaction:** The average customer review score was approximately 4.09 out of 5, indicating generally positive customer feedback.

6. **Delivery Performance:** Most orders were successfully delivered, while late and undelivered orders highlighted opportunities for operational improvement.

7. **Sales Trends:** Monthly and yearly sales analysis helped identify revenue patterns and potential seasonal variations.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing product categories through targeted promotions and inventory optimization.
- Expand business opportunities in geographical regions beyond the leading markets.
- Improve delivery performance by monitoring late and undelivered orders.
- Analyze negative customer reviews to identify service improvement opportunities.
- Optimize payment experiences by understanding customer payment preferences.
- Introduce personalized marketing campaigns to encourage repeat purchases.
